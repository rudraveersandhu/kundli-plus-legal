Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-22

Taara is published by [company name, to be registered] ("we"). Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. Questions, requests and complaints: privacy@example.com.

What Taara keeps on your phone

  • Your birth details: name, date, time and place of birth. Your Kundli is worked out from them on your phone.
  • People in your Circle: the names, relations, birth dates and (if you add them) birth places and times you enter.
  • What you do in the app: your Sadhana ticks, Drift Checks, reminder settings, language and chart style, and readings already written for you.

Birth details are stored encrypted (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). None of this leaves your phone except as described below. Your questions in Ask and Taara's answers are kept on your phone.

What is sent to our servers

To write readings (Blueprint, today's Compass, how you and someone fit, your Sadhana), the app sends chart facts only: signs, houses, nakshatras, planetary strengths, dasha periods, the day's panchang, Mool Ank numbers, the relation and label you chose for someone, and Milan scores. Never anyone's name, birth date, birth time or birth place.

These go to our server (Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India), which asks Anthropic's Claude to write the reading and stores it so it is written only once. Anthropic processes the facts in the United States to write the reading and does not use them to train its models.

To answer a question in Ask, the app sends your question, your last three questions with the short answers you got, and the same chart facts plus your notable days, dasha periods and sky events ahead. Our server passes them to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer. Our server keeps only that a question was asked (to count the daily limit), never the question or the answer.

To keep your readings yours, the app signs in with an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or phone number.

If you sign in (optional)

Taara works fully without an account. If you sign in with Google or Apple to back up and sync, we receive your email address from them, and your birth details, Circle, Sadhana, Drift Checks, Ask conversation, language, chart style and Milan side are stored on our server in Mumbai so another phone can pick up where you left off. They are stored encrypted. Reminders, your diagnostics answer and your agreement to this policy stay on each phone.

Signing out stops syncing; your details stay on the phone.

If you buy Taara Plus

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. RevenueCat (in the United States) receives the purchase record with your anonymous ID to keep track of your plan, and our server keeps whether you have Plus and until when.

Crash reports and usage counts (only if you agree)

If you say yes, the app sends:

  • Crash reports to Sentry (servers in the EU or the US): what went wrong in the app's code, the app version, the phone model and system version. Never your name, the phone's name, screenshots, or anything you typed.
  • Usage counts to our server in Mumbai, such as "finished setup", "opened Milan" or "asked a question" (without the question). They are linked only to your anonymous ID.

You can change your answer any time in Profile.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone other than the services named above. Taara doesn't use your location: panchang timings use your saved birth city.

People you add to your Circle

Only add someone's details if they're happy for you to. Their details stay on your phone; only chart facts (never their name or birth details) are sent to write how you two fit.

How long data is kept

Everything stays until you remove it. Profile → Delete my data deletes it all, from your phone and from our server, including your account if you signed in; RevenueCat's purchase record is deleted too. It can't be undone. A Taara Plus subscription is billed by Google Play or the App Store, so cancel it there first. You can also ask us to delete your data at privacy@example.com.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold about you at any time: Profile → Download my data gives you a file with the data on your phone and on our server. You can also ask us to correct or erase it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to privacy@example.com; we will reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

Taara is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will show the new version and ask again where your consent is needed.

Readings are for guidance and cultural insight, not medical, legal or financial advice.
